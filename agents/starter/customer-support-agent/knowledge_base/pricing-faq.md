# Pricing FAQ

## Plans

### Free Plan
- Up to 100 API calls per month
- Community support only
- Basic features
- No credit card required

### Pro Plan - $29/month
- Up to 10,000 API calls per month
- Email support (24-hour response time)
- All features including analytics dashboard
- Priority processing
- 14-day free trial available

### Enterprise Plan - Custom pricing
- Unlimited API calls
- Dedicated account manager
- Phone + email support (4-hour response time)
- Custom integrations
- SLA guarantees (99.9% uptime)
- Volume discounts available
- Contact sales@example.com for a quote

## Billing

- All plans are billed monthly, cancel anytime
- Annual billing available at 20% discount (Pro plan: $278/year instead of $348)
- Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month
- Payment methods: credit card, debit card, ACH bank transfer (Enterprise only)

## Upgrades and Downgrades

- You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from Settings > Billing
- Upgrades take effect immediately, and you are charged the prorated difference
- Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle
- No penalties or fees for changing plans

## Refund Policy for Subscriptions

- Full refund within the first 7 days of any new subscription
- No refunds after 7 days (you keep access until the end of the billing period)
- Enterprise contracts may have custom refund terms
