# Shipping Information

## Shipping Methods

| Method | Delivery Time | Cost |
|--------|--------------|------|
| Standard | 5-7 business days | Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99 |
| Express | 2-3 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $24.99 |

## Order Processing

- Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on business days are processed same-day
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST or on weekends/holidays are processed the next business day
- You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking number once your order ships

## Tracking Your Order

1. Check your email for the shipping confirmation
2. Click the tracking link, or go to dashboard.example.com and view "Order History"
3. You can also track directly at the carrier's website using the tracking number

## International Shipping

We ship to over 40 countries. International orders:
- Delivery takes 7-21 business days depending on destination
- Customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient
- Some items may not be available for international shipping
- International returns must be shipped at the customer's expense

## Shipping Issues

**Package not arrived within expected timeframe?**
- Check the tracking number first
- Allow 1-2 extra business days during peak seasons (November-December)
- Contact us if the package shows "delivered" but you haven't received it

**Damaged during shipping?**
- Take photos of the damage (both packaging and item)
- Contact us within 48 hours
- We will send a replacement at no additional cost
